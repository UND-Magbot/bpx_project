# MirrorMe 기술지원 문의 (초안)

받는 곳: support@mirrormetech.com
제목: BPX SDK integration questions (firmware 1.0.14, bpx_sdk_open 1.0.9)

Hello MirrorMe Technical Support,

We are integrating a BPX quadruped with ROS 2 Humble on an onboard mini PC (Ubuntu 22.04, x86_64),
connected over the rear RJ45 port (robot 10.21.20.1). Our setup:

- Robot software reported by the SDK: **1.0.14** (build 20260818)
- SDK: **bpx_sdk_open 1.0.9** (Python wheel, cp310 x86_64); also tested 1.0.8
- Control: `MotionLevelControl` (stand / sit / velocity). Stand, sit and walking work.

We would appreciate answers to the following questions.

**1. Rear "BAT Power Port" (GND / 33V)**
We plan to power a mini PC and a Livox Mid-360S LiDAR from this port through DC-DC converters.
- What is the maximum continuous and peak current of this port? Is it fused?
- Is the output the raw battery voltage (24.0–33.6 V)?
- Does the port stay powered when the robot is lying down, in damping, or after the E-Stop is pressed?
- What is the exact connector part number (it looks like an XT30-type with two signal pins)?

**2. Charger status flags**
`getChargerIn1()` / `getChargerIn2()` always return 0 on our robot, even while the charger is connected and
charging (battery current +2.38 A). What do In1 and In2 mean, and which firmware reports them correctly?

**3. Firmware version and missing feedback**
On firmware 1.0.14, `getControlMode()`, `getRobotSerialNumber()` and `getRobotModel()` return None.
Your Robonix hardware validation used robot software 1.0.15.
- What is the latest firmware version, and does it provide these values?
- Is updating through the BAOBOT app (System Version) the recommended procedure? Is there a changelog?

**4. Command priority**
What is the arbitration rule when the app, an S-Bus RC receiver and the SDK send commands at the same time?
Is there a way for an SDK client to detect that the app or RC has taken control (other than ControlMode)?

**5. Battery / BMS data**
- Please confirm `getBatteryCurrent()` is in amperes with positive = charging (we measured +2.38 A charging,
  −0.64 A idle lying down).
- Can the SDK provide pack voltage, cell voltages, battery temperature, cycle count or state of health?

**6. Units and limits**
- Unit of `getJointTorque()` (N·m?) and the IMU acceleration frame (we measured m/s², z up in `base_link`).
- Recommended warning / shutdown thresholds for motor and driver temperatures (°C).
- While charging, `getMaxVelocity()` returns [0, 0, 0] and stand-up requests are ignored. Is that the intended
  charging lock, and are there other conditions that lock motion?

Thank you for your support.

Best regards,
Noah
(BPX ROS 2 integration)
